Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the $700 landing page?

One custom responsive page, technical SEO foundations, a contact or lead form, performance checks and deployment to your domain.

Why do custom platforms start at $3,000?

They may require databases, secure APIs, user accounts, dashboards, bookings, uploads or integrations. The final quote depends on which capabilities are required.

Will I own the website?

Yes. After final payment, the completed website and project code are handed over without proprietary platform lock-in.

Where will the website be hosted?

Static websites are normally deployed on Cloudflare Pages. Applications can add Cloudflare Workers and databases when server-side functionality is needed.
